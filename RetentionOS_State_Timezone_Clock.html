⚠ Arizona doesn't observe Daylight Saving Time

Arizona stays on standard Mountain Time year-round. In winter (standard time), it matches Colorado exactly. In summer (DST active elsewhere), Colorado springs forward and Arizona doesn't — so Colorado runs one hour ahead of Arizona for roughly eight months of the year, despite both being "Mountain."

⚠ Tennessee is split across two timezones

Middle and West Tennessee (Nashville, Memphis) run Central Time — shown below. East Tennessee (Knoxville, Chattanooga, the Tri-Cities) runs Eastern Time instead. Confirm which region a specific lead is in before relying on this board alone.
